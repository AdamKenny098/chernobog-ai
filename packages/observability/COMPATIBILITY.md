# Compatibility

OH-1M preserves the accepted Phase 11 diagnostic estate and the existing Chernobog Trust Trace output. Existing producers may continue emitting their current shapes while new cross-domain interfaces converge on @chernobog/observability.

This slice does not implement the OH-9 observability redesign. It also does not introduce the OH-1N composition root; OH-1N may inject observability facilities later.
